ELLIOTT, OCCASIONALLY HOLDING STILL

I am Elliott. I'm an Adelaide-based creative director, filmmaker and producer who likes seeing what an idea can become.

I built Green Door with a small team because I wanted a reason for strangers to get together. I make films because some things are easier to feel than explain. And when I notice something at work that could run better, I have a hard time leaving it alone.

I do a lot myself, from the first rough sketch to shooting, editing, set design, campaigns and the little systems that hold everything together. I'm not an agency with a department for everything. I'm one person who knows how to get things moving, and who to call when it needs more hands.

I work with briefs, budgets and actual deadlines. I care about how something looks, but more about whether it works for the people it's meant for.

03 / HOW I WORK

I dream it up.
I figure it out.
I make it fucking real.

A good idea can start anywhere. A phone note. A half-built set. A problem at my part-time job. I take it apart, work out what it needs, then get moving.

I can shoot, edit, write, plan, design a space and put together a campaign myself. When a project needs other people, I work with people who are better at their part than I am.
