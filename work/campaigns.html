01 / THE APPROACH

Give people something to get curious about.

I’m not interested in filling a feed for the sake of it. I’d rather build a visual idea that feels like it belongs somewhere, then give people a reason to follow it into real life.

Each gathering gets its own campaign world, shoot concepts, visual cues, stories, partner content and release rhythm. It all has to work with the time, budget and people we actually have.

02 / BEYOND VIEWS

The right people, not just more people.

Our audience is deliberately local. In the November 2026 Instagram audience snapshot, 74.8% of Green Door’s followers were based in Adelaide and 83.9% were aged 18 to 34.

We used film, recurring visual formats, The Welcome Mat, partner posts, targeted promotion and Humanitix to move from being seen to getting people through the door. Gathering IV sold out its ticket allocation.

There’s still plenty to refine. That’s the point of actually measuring things rather than assuming a nice-looking post did its job.
