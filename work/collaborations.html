THE GOAL

Make the partnership matter online and in the room

01 / THE IDEA

Make them part of the story.

A collaboration should do more than put two names on the same post. We wanted the people involved to have their own part in the world we were building, and something worth sharing with their audiences.

I built simple film concepts around who they were, what they brought, and what we could realistically shoot. Good ideas, a few hours together, the right props. Most of our campaign films are shot in one short session and cut on a tight turnaround.

02 / THE EXCHANGE

We make each other better.

I can offer concepts, production and content that small local creatives might not have the time or tools to make themselves. They bring talent, people, personality and a reason for their audience to pay attention.

Then we put it to use together at the gatherings. The online story has a point, because the real-world experience is waiting at the other end.
